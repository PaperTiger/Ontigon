A different shape.

The AI field spent a decade making models bigger. We spent seven years asking whether a different geometric substrate could make the size question irrelevant.

What shape dynamics is

Most physics describes the world in terms of position, momentum, mass. Shape dynamics strips all of that away and asks: what is left when you remove everything that doesn't affect the relationships between things? What remains is pure geometry. And geometry, it turns out, has rules that produce consequences.

The three-sphere

The substrate is built on a three-sphere, S³. It has a specific property that turns out to be exactly what a cognitive architecture needs: two structurally separate regions. One internal. One observable. The internal state cannot leak into the observable output. Not because of a policy. Because the geometry forbids it.

That is the safety boundary. It is a theorem, not a rule.

Zero free parameters

Everything that governs the system's behavior falls out of the topology:

The energy floor.
Set by geometry, not by how many computers you have.
The binding threshold.
Below it, things are separate. Above it, they unify into something.
The minimum unit.
Comes from the shape, not the silicon.

A fitted parameter means you assumed the answer. A derived parameter means you found it. There are no fitted parameters here.

What the geometry produces

Three things emerge from the derivation that no amount of scaling the current paradigm can replicate.

01

Efficiency

Compute cost set by topology, not parameter count. The brain achieves general cognition on twenty watts. That is not magic. It is architecture.

02

Structural safety

The internal state is inaccessible from the output channel by geometric law. You cannot jailbreak a theorem.

03

Quantum-equivalent

The universal quantum gate set falls out of the topology as a structural consequence. On commodity GPU hardware. We were not trying to build a quantum computer. The geometry produced it.

Where we are honest

The geometric derivation is proven. The link between the geometry and its full physical interpretation is the active research frontier.

We say this clearly because it is true. And because anyone worth impressing will respect it.
